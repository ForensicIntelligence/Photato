## Test material

Layer alignment/distribution uses an independent Swift implementation informed by PhotoCraft `crates/engine/src/layer_multi_cmds.rs` and original Photoshop 2026 experiments. The 26 single-operation input/reference pairs and one consecutive-command reference in `Tests/PhotoCraftImagingTests/Fixtures/layout` contain only original procedural rectangles, licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0. Their generators, source hashes and limits are recorded in `docs/LAYER_LAYOUT_REFERENCES.json` and development steps 053–054. The core expected-position fixture is independently decoded from those saved files. These fixtures are excluded from the app bundle; no Adobe code, preset or artwork is included.

The gradient oracles execute the original Rust geometry, interpolation, opacity, HSB conversion, dither, GRD and descriptor code. `docs/GRADIENT_ORACLE.json`, `docs/GRADIENT_PRESET_ORACLE.json` and `docs/GRADIENT_TOOL_ORACLE.json` record source and generated fixture hashes. These algorithm/format fixtures retain MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms and are excluded from the app. The installed Photoshop default and legacy GRD libraries were inspected read-only, including their `phry` folder extensions; no installed preset data or rendered samples are copied into the source or app. The gradient atlas uses original test colors.

The pattern oracle executes original Rust sampling, identity, procedural preset and PAT/PackBits methods with standalone storage adapters. Hashes are in `docs/PATTERN_ORACLE.json`. Generated algorithm fixtures retain MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms; procedural tile pixels are upstream CC0. Installed Photoshop PAT libraries were inspected read-only for compatibility; those installed-library files and pixels are not copied into the source or application bundle. Reports from that inspection retain metadata and hashes only.

The focus/smudge oracle executes the original Rust numerical methods with minimal standalone storage adapters. Source and fixture hashes are in `docs/FOCUS_ORACLE.json`. This generated test data uses upstream MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms and is excluded from the app bundle.

The color-region oracle executes the original Rust `wand_region`, using its sequential row loops and standalone storage adapters. Source and fixture hashes are in `docs/COLOR_REGION_ORACLE.json`. Its 240 cases retain upstream MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms and are excluded from the app bundle.

The toning oracle executes unmodified Rust numerical functions, with only serde traits removed from the standalone harness. `docs/TONING_ORACLE.json` records the source and fixture hashes. These generated test vectors use the upstream MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms and are excluded from the app bundle.

The inpainting oracle is generated by executing the original Rust numerical functions with inert cancellation/progress and their existing sequential scheduling paths. The fixture covers patch completion, texture synthesis and proximity matching; `docs/INPAINT_ORACLE.json` records source and output hashes. It is generated test data under the upstream MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms and is excluded from the app bundle.

The source distribution includes a Photoshop-rasterized derivative of psd-tools `mask.psd`, under the MIT license in `Tests/PhotoCraftImagingTests/Fixtures/LICENSE-psd-tools`. `provenance.json` in that directory records origin, preparation and hashes. The feather and 16/32-bit precision fixtures are original generated test data, re-saved with Photoshop. PSD compression vectors are original deterministic Python/zlib data; their generator and provenance are included. Test files are not embedded in the app. Downloaded reference corpus files retain their upstream licenses and are excluded from version control.

Eight additional unmodified psd-tools adjustment PSDs are bundled only with the tests, under the same MIT notice: four Hue/Saturation references and four RGB/8 and RGB/16 Threshold/Posterize references. Their hashes and pinned commit are recorded in `artifacts/psd-adjustment-fixture-manifest.json` and `artifacts/psd-tonal-step-fixture-manifest.json`; `scripts/fetch-adjustment-fixtures.py` reproduces them. Their metadata records Photoshop 27.1 (Windows) saves. They supply independent saved-composite and format references; no Adobe preset, application code or branded assets are included. Colorize's corrected lightness ordering is independently implemented in Swift and checked against these pixels; psd-tools' documented HSL processing order was also consulted. Threshold and high-depth Posterize use equations checked against the saved references; psd-tools’ Threshold rounding was also consulted; their remaining pixel differences are recorded explicitly.

Three unmodified pattern PSD fixtures from the MIT-licensed psd-tools repository are included in the test resources. Their pinned commit, hashes and paths are recorded in `artifacts/psd-pattern-fixture-manifest.json`; `scripts/fetch-pattern-fixtures.py` reproduces them. These PSDs contain embedded pattern sample pixels, including Adobe-named presets from their original documents. They are interoperability fixtures, not Photato presets, and are excluded from the application bundle. The earlier installed-PAT inspection did not copy any installed Adobe libraries. The linked fill-frame placement adapter follows PhotoCraft `crates/compose/src/lib.rs`; the native implementation is covered by the upstream MIT OR Apache-2.0 attribution.

Five additional unmodified gradient PSD fixtures from the same MIT-licensed psd-tools repository are included only in test resources. Their pinned commit, hashes and creator metadata are recorded in `artifacts/psd-gradient-fixture-manifest.json`. A Photoshop CC 2018 gradient fixture supplies an exact stored-channel reference; its minimal companion has a blank white merged preview. The source-derived mask-frame and canvas-angle models come from `crates/compose/src/lib.rs` and `crates/engine/src/canvas_geom.rs`. PSD merged white matting follows the format behavior documented by `crates/io/src/pixels.rs`, independently qualified against saved fixture channels. No test fixtures are packaged into Photato.

Five Photoshop 2026 Gradient Map PSDs were authored through the available app from an original deterministic RGBA probe. They contain generated test colors, a black-to-white map and Photoshop-generated document metadata; no installed gradient libraries were copied. `docs/GRADIENT_MAP_FIXTURES.json` records hashes and acquisition details. `scripts/generate-gradient-map-probe.py` reproduces the source PNG, and `scripts/inspect-gradient-map-reference.py` independently inspects saved records and merged channels. These original interoperability fixtures are test resources under this project’s MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms and are excluded from the app. One preserved native export was opened in Photoshop to inspect editable controls; it is not a pixel-equivalence reference.

Seven further Photoshop-saved original probes qualify Stripes Map/Fill behavior and record unimplemented Smooth behavior; an eighth native RGB32 fixture verifies legacy retention and export rejection. `docs/GRADIENT_STRIPES_FIXTURES.json` records every hash, preserved native input and independent recalculation check. These are original generated interoperability fixtures under the project’s MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms, excluded from Photato. No installed presets or Adobe implementation code are copied.

An additional original 65,536-pixel RGB16 alpha/color matrix was recalculated and saved through Photoshop 2026 to qualify merged transparency rounding. Its generator, original input and saved reference hashes are recorded in `docs/RGB16_MATTE_REFERENCES.json`. The fixture uses MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms, is excluded from the app bundle, and contains no user image or copied Adobe implementation.

`Tests/PhotoCraftImagingTests/Fixtures/gradient-map-stop-limits.json` contains original procedural Gradient Map records and records extracted from their Photoshop 2026 saves. The RGB16 Gradient Map tone fixtures contain 131,072 measured tone indices and three original 65,536-pixel Stripes documents saved by Photoshop 2026, including reverse and varying alpha. The tone vectors are extracted from saved identity outputs, independently of the native coefficient equation. `docs/GRADIENT_MAP_TONE_REFERENCES.json` records provenance and hashes. The companion 224 RGB8/float vectors capture the preceding native behavior and are regression data, not Photoshop references. All are original generated data under MIT OR Apache-2.0, excluded from the app bundle; no user imagery, Adobe source or installed presets are included.

The Classic RGB16 corpus contains 21 original procedural gradient references and four original Photoshop-saved PSDs. Expected lookup samples were extracted independently from saved merged pixels; no candidate renderer generated the expectations. The native arithmetic is an independent implementation derived from those observations. `docs/CLASSIC_GRADIENT_RESEARCH.json` records provenance, hashes and the two unresolved one-level sample positions. These test fixtures use MIT OR Apache-2.0 and are excluded from the app bundle.

The Layer Via fixtures contain original procedural source/copy/cut documents saved through Photoshop 2026. Three alpha-plane matrices independently qualify 196,608 source/selection pairs across RGB/8, RGB/16 and RGB/32. The 27 small PSD companions remove only the saved selection channel and its resource metadata, retaining all layer records/pixels; full originals and derivation hashes are recorded in `docs/LAYER_VIA_REFERENCES.json`. No Adobe code, installed presets or user pixels are copied. These original interoperability fixtures use MIT OR Apache-2.0 and are excluded from the app bundle.

## Changes from upstream

The native implementation lives in `Sources/`; tests, packaging and documentation here are new or adapted for this fork. It replaces the Rust application/engine with Swift, AppKit, SwiftUI, CoreGraphics, CoreText and ImageIO, with optional system AI integration. See `docs/DEVLOG.md` and `docs/STATUS.md` for the implemented scope and known gaps. The original Rust snapshot in the sibling directory has not been modified.

The layer-merge corpus contains original procedural RGB8/16/32 two-layer color/alpha matrices and small off-canvas/hidden-layer workflow documents saved by Photoshop 2026. Four independent matrices supply 262,144 exact raw RGBA kernel expectations. The fixtures, independent extraction scripts and newly derived Swift arithmetic use MIT OR Apache-2.0 terms; no Adobe implementation code or user artwork is included. Provenance, hashes and limits are recorded in `docs/LAYER_MERGE_REFERENCES.json`. Test fixtures are excluded from the app bundle.
