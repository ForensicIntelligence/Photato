# Source checkpoint 573

Native Swift 0.3.0, build 573, 18 November 2026. When a Normal-layer color contribution falls below the Float32 normal range, the Intel renderer now writes a signed zero before the contribution is added to the backdrop. It also clears subnormal results from that addition. The product check includes values below the boundary that round up to the smallest normal number. The Apple Silicon path keeps its fused operation. For an ordinary unmasked opaque source that fills the canvas, the renderer copies straight samples over uniform backdrop alpha. If the backdrop varies, it interpolates. Grouped clipping copies eligible source colors and keeps the existing alpha of its base layer.

The correction is in the shared RGB32 Normal renderer used by canvas composition and the PSD and PSB preview writers. A regression reads two owned layered PSDs and compares 11,664 straight RGBA words per architecture with the corresponding Photoshop 27.2.0 output. Both layers have positive coverage at those positions. The separately compiled Intel build 572 differed at 7,012 of the same words. Other regressions check 27,648 words from twelve opacity and geometry probes and 18,192 words from sixteen backdrop-alpha and clipping probes. The current Apple Silicon and Intel builds match their separate Photoshop captures in all 57,504 words.

All 708 selected tests passed in Debug, optimized Apple Silicon and optimized Intel under Rosetta. Both slices of the extracted local validation archive passed signature and offscreen workspace checks. The archive declares macOS 14 as its minimum version. Testing on macOS 14 and on physical Intel hardware remains open.

Extreme colors over an alpha-bearing backdrop can produce infinite words in Photoshop. Photato continues to report a composition error for that range. The translucent fixture also exposed a separate difference in the desktop and API merge preparation. The canvas renderer matches Photoshop Merge Visible on all 5,184 verified words, while Photato Merge Visible differs in 98 RGB words on Apple Silicon and 3,198 under Rosetta. Very low alpha, independent clipping and complete Photoshop workflow parity remain open.
